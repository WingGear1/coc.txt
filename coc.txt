# SKYHIGH CODE OF CONDUCT

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

**1.1**
This Code of Conduct establishes the minimum standards of behaviour expected from all individuals using the SkyHigh Network, including pilots, air traffic controllers, staff members, and guests.

**1.2**
This Code applies at all times while connected to the SkyHigh Network or using any official SkyHigh services or platforms, including Discord servers, forums, training systems, and official events.

**1.3**
Violation of this Code may result in warnings, suspension, restriction of privileges, or permanent removal from the SkyHigh Network, as outlined in the Code of Regulations.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 2. GENERAL CONDUCT

**2.1**
Members shall conduct themselves in a professional, respectful, and courteous manner towards all other members and staff.

**2.2**
Harassment, discrimination, abusive behaviour, threats, toxic conduct, or profanity used in an abusive, disruptive, or inappropriate manner is strictly prohibited regardless of platform or communication method.

**2.3**
Members shall comply with all lawful instructions issued by authorised SkyHigh staff while connected to the network or participating in official services.

**2.4**
Members shall not engage in conduct that damages, or is likely to damage, the reputation, stability, or operation of SkyHigh.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 3. ACCOUNTS AND IDENTITY

**3.1**
Each individual may hold only one SkyHigh membership account unless otherwise authorised in writing by SkyHigh staff.

**3.2**
Members must register using their real legal first and last name as used in everyday life for identification and moderation purposes.

**3.3**
Members shall not share their account credentials with any other individual or access the network using another person’s account.

**3.4**
Members remain responsible for all activity conducted under their account, regardless of whether the activity was authorised by them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 4. VOICE AND TEXT COMMUNICATIONS

**4.1**
Operational communication shall be conducted in clear and concise English unless local procedures explicitly permit another language.

**4.2**
Members shall maintain professional and operationally relevant communications while connected to the network.

**4.3**
Voice and text channels used for operational purposes shall remain relevant to aviation operations.

**4.4**
Recording or streaming another member’s transmissions or voice communications must comply with applicable laws and SkyHigh privacy guidance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 5. PILOT CONDUCT

**5.1**
Pilots shall operate aircraft in a manner consistent with real-world aviation practices while remaining within the limitations of their simulator and experience level.

**5.2**
Pilots shall comply with valid ATC instructions and clearances unless doing so would compromise simulator safety.

**5.3**
Pilots must continuously monitor assigned frequencies and maintain the ability to transmit and receive communications while connected to the voice environment.

**5.4**
Intentional disruption, including reckless flying, runway incursions, taxiway obstruction, or repeated disconnecting and reconnecting to gain advantage, is prohibited.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 6. AIR TRAFFIC CONTROLLER CONDUCT

**6.1**
Controllers shall provide service professionally using phraseology and procedures consistent with real-world operations and SkyHigh training standards.

**6.2**
Controllers shall not operate positions or provide services beyond their authorised rating or endorsements.

**6.3**
Controllers must remain attentive and available while connected.

**6.4**
Controllers shall coordinate effectively with neighbouring sectors and facilities to maintain orderly and realistic operations.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 7. COMMUNITY PLATFORMS

**7.1**
Official SkyHigh community platforms, including Discord servers and forums, are considered extensions of the network environment and remain fully subject to this Code.

**7.2**
Sharing explicit, hateful, violent, or otherwise inappropriate content is prohibited.

**7.3**
Members shall respect moderators and staff while complying with all platform-specific rules and procedures.

**7.4**
Public disputes, harassment campaigns, or “name-and-shame” posts targeting individuals are not permitted on official SkyHigh platforms.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 8. CHEATING, EXPLOITATION, AND TECHNICAL ABUSE

**8.1**
Members shall not use software, scripts, exploits, or unauthorised modifications to gain unfair advantages or disrupt network operations.

**8.2**
Attempting to bypass technical restrictions, disciplinary actions, or network security measures is prohibited.

**8.3**
Members shall not intentionally transmit false, manipulated, or misleading network data.

**8.4**
Unauthorised access to, interference with, or modification of SkyHigh systems is strictly prohibited.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 9. REAL-WORLD REFERENCES AND PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES

**9.1**
SkyHigh is a virtual simulation network and must not be used to provide real-world aviation instructions or operational control.

**9.2**
Members shall not falsely imply endorsement by real-world aviation authorities, airlines, airports, or regulators unless officially authorised by SkyHigh.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 10. REPORTING AND ENFORCEMENT

**10.1**
Members are encouraged to report suspected violations through official SkyHigh reporting systems or designated staff channels.

**10.2**
SkyHigh staff may collect logs, voice recordings, screenshots, chat history, and other relevant evidence during investigations.

**10.3**
Disciplinary action may include warnings, suspensions, permanent bans, role restrictions, or removal of network privileges in accordance with the Code of Regulations.
